PhantomKingdom

PhantomKingdom is an open-source Android reimplementation of Pocket Kingdom: Own the World. It lets you play offline or connect to a PhantomLink server for online play. You must import game data from your own copy; the app does not include or download it.

PhantomKingdom is not affiliated with SEGA or Nokia.
