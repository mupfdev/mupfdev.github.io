PhantomKingdom privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy describes data handled by PhantomKingdom for Android, an open-source reimplementation of Pocket Kingdom. You can play offline or use a PhantomLink server for online play. The app does not include or download the original game data.

Game data and local storage

The game files you import are copied into the app's private storage and are not uploaded by the app. Imported game data is excluded from Android backup. You can remove imported data in the app; files copied over USB remain where you put them.

The app stores saves, settings, a randomly generated N-Gage ID and Arena account details on your device. These may be included in Android device backups, depending on your settings. An app event log is held in memory and shared only if you choose to share it.

Save exports are written to the location you choose. A save backup contains an encrypted save and the N-Gage ID needed to decrypt it; the ZIP itself is not encrypted. An N-Gage export contains the encrypted save and its separate salt. The app does not upload these files, but your chosen storage provider may handle them under its own terms.

PhantomLink online play

When you connect to N-Gage Arena, the app sends your Arena user name and password, the generated N-Gage ID, kingdom and match data, and your IP address to the PhantomLink server you select. The server uses the ID to bind the account to your installation. Other players may see your kingdom name and game information. The server test only opens and closes a connection.

The original game protocol is not encrypted. A server may be operated by a third party, whose own privacy practices apply; choose a server you trust.

Google Play Games Services

Google Play Games Services is planned for a future Google Play build and is not included in the GitHub APK. When enabled in a future build, Google may authenticate your Play Games profile and process profile information, achievement progress and leaderboard scores to provide those features. Leaderboard entries may be visible to other players. Google processes this information under its privacy policy and Play Games settings.

Planned features include achievements for boss treasures and a leaderboard for Ranked player-versus-player wins. The app will not require Play Games sign-in for ordinary gameplay. This section will be updated if the implemented features or data handling differ.

Permissions and choices

Internet access is used for online play and the server test; a Play Games-enabled build would also use it for Play Games. Vibration provides game feedback. The app uses Android's file picker rather than requesting broad storage access.

You can play offline without connecting to a PhantomLink server. Uninstalling the app removes its private data, but not separately exported files or data held by a server or Google. To manage or request deletion of data held by a PhantomLink server, contact its operator. Manage your Play Games profile and game data through Google's settings or Google's support.

Children and contact

The app is not directed at children under 13.
